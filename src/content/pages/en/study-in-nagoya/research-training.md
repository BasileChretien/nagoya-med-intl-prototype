The Program for International Research Students lets students from abroad work for a short period in a laboratory of Nagoya University Graduate School of Medicine. The number of students the school can accept is limited, so contact the Office of International Affairs before you prepare an application.

## Who can apply

- You are an enrolled undergraduate or graduate student at a fully accredited school in your own country.
- You are in good standing and have a written letter of approval from the Dean of your school or the Dean's authorized representative.
- You communicate fluently in Japanese or English. A TOEFL iBT score above 100 is desirable if English is not your first language.
- You do not take side jobs during the program.

## Length of stay and certificate

The programme lasts one week to four weeks. If you want to stay longer, ask the office. Students who come through NUPACE, the university-wide exchange program, can stay from 91 days to one year.

You receive a certificate of attendance but no credits. Your home school decides how to process the certificate.

## Finding a laboratory

The Graduate School publishes a directory of its laboratories for prospective international students. Browse it, then describe the research that interests you in your motivation letter and in your first enquiry to the Office of International Affairs.

## Fees, scholarship and insurance

Tuition is 29,600 yen for each four weeks, paid when the programme starts. Registration and admission fees are waived on the basis of the Dean's letter. Students of partner schools that have a tuition waiver agreement with Nagoya University pay no tuition, registration or admission fees.

NUPACE students who are full-time students at their home school and stay 91 days to one year can apply for a JASSO scholarship of 80,000 yen a month, plus a one-time allowance of 80,000 yen after arrival. Places are very limited.

Everyone must hold liability insurance for injury or damage to third parties and show proof. If you cannot, you can buy the insurance used by Nagoya University School of Medicine students for 1,723 yen a year. NUPACE students pay monthly premiums to Japan National Health Insurance and buy Student Personal Liability Insurance from the Nagoya University Co-operative. Other students need a health insurance policy that covers their stay, and must show proof.

## How to apply

1. Write to the Office of International Affairs with your name, your school and its country, and the dates you intend to stay.
2. After the office confirms availability, prepare the documents below.
3. NUPACE applicants send the documents with the NUPACE application by the date on the NUPACE website. All other applicants send them to the Office of International Affairs of the School of Medicine, not to the NUPACE Office, six months before the start.

### Documents

- Application form for visiting students in laboratories
- Letter of recommendation from your Dean or the Dean's representative, stating that you are in good standing
- Proof of personal health insurance (not required for NUPACE applicants)
- Immunization certificate signed by a physician, only if you plan to stay more than 31 days. It confirms a negative TB screening and immunity to rubella and measles.
- Motivation letter with your study plan
- Photograph data, 2.5 cm by 3 cm
- Passport copy
- Official certificate of enrolment, or a copy of your student ID
- Official transcript
