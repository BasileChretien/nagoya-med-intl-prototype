Nagoya University Graduate School of Medicine welcomes students from abroad for two kinds of visit. Senior medical students can join a clinical clerkship at Nagoya University Hospital. Undergraduate and graduate students can take part in research training in a laboratory of the Graduate School.

Rotations are coordinated in English. The Office of International Affairs arranges your rotation and looks after your stay, from the first enquiry to your return home. Students from partner institutions may have tuition waived, and a dormitory is available when there are vacancies.

Clinical clerkships are currently open to students of partner medical schools only. If you are not sure whether your school is a partner, check the list of partner universities on this site or ask the office before you prepare any documents.

## Which page is for you

- **Clinical clerkship.** For senior medical students of partner schools who want to join clinical rotations. It covers eligibility, length of stay, fees, insurance, documents and the application deadline.
- **Research training.** For enrolled undergraduate or graduate students who want to work in a laboratory. It covers the same points for research visits.
- **Living in Nagoya.** For students who are preparing to arrive or who are already here. It covers housing, visa, insurance, Japanese lessons, support from the office and how to reach the campus.
- **FAQ.** Short answers to the questions applicants ask most often.

Whichever programme you choose, begin by writing to the Office of International Affairs. The number of places is limited, so the office confirms availability before you send any application documents.
