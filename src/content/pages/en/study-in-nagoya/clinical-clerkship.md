Nagoya University School of Medicine accepts a limited number of international students in the clinical clerkships (rotations) of its senior students. At present only students of partner schools are accepted. To find out whether your school is one, see [Agreements and partner institutions](../partnerships/agreements). Please do not contact departments or faculty members directly. All enquiries go through the Office of International Affairs.

## When to apply

Applications are accepted all year round from students of partner schools. Write to the office first, because places are limited. Then send the complete documents by the deadline in the box at the top of this page. If you come through [NUPACE](https://nupace.iee.nagoya-u.ac.jp/en/index.shtml), the university-wide exchange program, follow the dates on the NUPACE website instead.

## Eligibility

- You are an enrolled medical student at a fully accredited medical school in your own country, in your senior year, with enough clinical clerkship experience at your school.
- You are in good standing and have a written letter of approval from the Dean of your school or the Dean's authorized representative.
- You communicate fluently in Japanese or English. A TOEFL iBT score above 100 is desirable if English is not your first language.
- You do not take side jobs during the clerkship.

If you have already graduated, see [Visiting researchers, postdocs and PhD applicants](visiting-researchers).

## Length of stay and certificate

Students of partner schools who apply directly can stay from one week to 90 days, within three rotations. You must spend at least two weeks in one department.

Students who apply through NUPACE stay from 91 days to one year. The clinical clerkship is limited to three months, and the rest of the stay can be other study, including a research project.

You receive a certificate of attendance but no credits. Your home school decides how to process the certificate. If you need another copy or a letter about your clerkship later, write to the office with your name, your home school, your dates and your departments.

## Fees, scholarship and housing

Tuition, registration and admission fees are waived for students of partner schools that have a tuition waiver agreement with Nagoya University. The number of waivers is limited. Otherwise you pay the tuition shown in the box at the top of this page for each four weeks, when the clerkship starts. Registration and admission fees are then waived on the basis of the Dean's letter.

NUPACE students who are full-time students at their home school can apply for a JASSO scholarship through NUPACE. The amount is in the box at the top of this page. Very few places are available.

A dormitory is available when there are vacancies. Ask the office when you apply.

### What else to budget for

- Malpractice insurance, if your own does not cover you (see below).
- Health insurance for your whole stay.
- Housing. One student who stayed in the Yamate international residence in 2024 paid about 26,000 yen a month in rent, plus 3,000 to 4,000 yen for electricity ([read the report](../stories/clerkship-2024-report180)). A private single room in Nagoya usually costs 35,000 to 60,000 yen a month, according to the [university's housing page](https://en.nagoya-u.ac.jp/campus/life/housing/).
- Travel to Japan, food and local transport. Ask the office if you need an estimate.

## Insurance

- **Malpractice insurance.** Everyone must hold cover for a clerkship in a foreign medical school, up to one million US dollars or more, and show proof. If you cannot, you can buy the insurance used by Nagoya University students for 1,723 yen a year.
- **Health insurance.** NUPACE students pay monthly premiums to Japan National Health Insurance and buy Student Personal Liability Insurance from the Nagoya University Co-operative. Other students need a health insurance policy that covers their stay, and must show proof.

## How to apply

1. Write to the Office of International Affairs before you send anything. Give your name, your school and its country, and the dates you want.
2. Once the office confirms availability, send the complete set of documents.
3. NUPACE applicants attach their documents to the NUPACE application and send both forms to the NUPACE Office. All other applicants send their documents to the Office of International Affairs of the School of Medicine, not to the NUPACE Office.

### Forms to download

- [Application for Visiting Student Clerkship](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/english/abroad/TemplateApplicationforVisitingStudentClerkship.pdf) (PDF, 447 KB)
- [Immunization requirements](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_E/international/pdf/immunization_requirements.pdf) (PDF, 213 KB), for your physician
- COVID-19 vaccination certificate: the official clerkship page still lists it. Ask the office whether you need it.

### Documents

- Clinical clerkship application form (above)
- Letter of recommendation from your Dean or the Dean's representative, stating that you are a senior student in good standing with at least half a year of clinical clerkship experience
- Proof of malpractice insurance
- Proof of personal health insurance (not required for NUPACE applicants)
- Immunization certificate signed by a physician, confirming a negative TB screening and immunity to rubella and measles
- Motivation letter with your study plan for the electives
- Photograph data, 2.5 cm by 3 cm
- Passport copy
- Official certificate of enrolment, or a copy of your student ID
- Official transcript
- CV in any format

## Before you arrive

Ask the office which visa or status of entry applies to your stay before you book your travel. The Office of International Affairs takes care of your rotation and your stay. [Living in Nagoya](living-in-nagoya) covers housing, visa, insurance, what to do on arrival and daily life, and [How to reach the office](../about/access) gives the map and directions.
