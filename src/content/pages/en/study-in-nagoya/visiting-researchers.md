The [research training programme](research-training) is for students who are still enrolled at their home school. If you have graduated, for example as a doctor, a postdoctoral researcher or a researcher, this page shows the routes that are open to you. All of them start with a laboratory that agrees to host you.

## Find a host laboratory first

1. Browse the [laboratory directory](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_E/laboratory/) of the Graduate School. Open a laboratory, then the name of its professor, to see an email address.
2. Write to the professor. Describe the research you want to do and attach your CV.
3. When the professor agrees to host you, follow the route below that fits your situation.

Some professors do not publish their email address. In that case, write to the Student Affairs Division of the Graduate School, using the address on the [research student guidelines](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_E/admission/research/guideline/).

This is different from the clinical clerkship, where you do not contact departments yourself and every enquiry goes through the Office of International Affairs.

## Research student: research without a degree

A research student enrols at the Graduate School to do research on a set topic, without working towards a degree. Each school of Nagoya University sets its own rules for research students, so follow the Graduate School of Medicine's page.

1. Get the agreement of a professor who will host you.
2. Send these documents by email to the Student Affairs Division of the Graduate School of Medicine:
   - the application form for research students (an Excel file on the faculty page)
   - your CV, in any format
   - your research proposal, in any format
   - a copy of your graduation certificate, or of your certificate of expected graduation (both undergraduate and graduate certificates if you hold a graduate degree)
   - a copy of your passport
   - a copy of your residence card, only if you already live in Japan
3. If your files are larger than 5 megabytes in total, share them through a file-sharing service instead of attaching them.

The current form, the email address and the steps are on the faculty's [research student guidelines](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_E/admission/research/guideline/) ([Japanese page](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_J/admission/research/foreign/)). Fees and housing for research students are explained on the university's [research students page](https://en.nagoya-u.ac.jp/admissions/research-students/research-students/).

If you come from abroad as a research student, ask the Student Affairs Division which status of residence applies. The [visa section of Living in Nagoya](living-in-nagoya) explains the Certificate of Eligibility that the university requests for students.

## Postdocs and visiting researchers

If you come with your own funding or appointment, agree on your project and dates with your host professor first. For the next steps, including your status of residence and the papers you need, ask the Office of International Affairs through the [contact page](../about/contact).

Nagoya University helps international researchers with housing, visas, language and orientation. See [researcher support](https://en.nagoya-u.ac.jp/campus/researcher-support/).

## Doctoral course (PhD) for applicants from abroad

The Graduate School of Medicine admits doctoral students in April and in October. The faculty's [doctoral admission page](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_E/admission/doctral/admissions/general/) gives the dates, documents and guidelines for each intake. The laboratory directory marks the laboratories that accept doctoral students, but a laboratory may not take new students every year, so ask your future supervisor before you apply.

### MEXT scholarship through a Japanese embassy

The Japanese government (MEXT) offers scholarships to research students. In the embassy route, you apply through the Japanese embassy or consulate in your country.

- Embassies recruit in April and May of the year before arrival, and hold the first screening between May and July.
- Scholarship students arrive in Japan in April, or in September or October.
- The process differs from country to country, so ask the Japanese embassy or consulate in your country.

[Study in Japan](https://www.studyinjapan.go.jp/en/smap-stopj-applications-research.html) explains the embassy route.

### Medical Science Research Leader programme (MEXT, university recommendation)

The Graduate School runs a special programme selected by MEXT, the PhD Program for Fostering Medical Science Research Leaders. Through it, the university recommends international students for a MEXT scholarship and a place in the doctoral course.

- You need a GPA of 2.30 or higher on the MEXT scale.
- Before you apply, ask the laboratory you want to join about your research project and get its approval.
- Applicants who already live in Japan may also apply.
- Students enter in October.

For entry on 1 October 2027, the faculty page gives these dates: applications from 5 to 13 January 2027, and an interview in late January 2027. The deadline for the eligibility review, for applicants who need one, was 11 November 2026 and has passed. Check the faculty's [doctoral admission page](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_E/admission/doctral/admissions/general/) for the guidelines and forms.

### Joint and double degrees

Nagoya University runs joint degree and double degree programmes with universities abroad. In a joint degree, the two universities award one degree in their joint names. In a double degree, each university awards its own degree. See [Degrees](../degrees) for the programmes and who can apply.

## Students of other universities in Japan

The clinical clerkship and the research training on this site are written for students of schools abroad. If you study at another university in Japan and want a short clinical or research stay, write to the Office of International Affairs before you prepare anything. For a longer research stay, the research student route above also takes applicants who live in Japan.

## If your family comes with you

The Nagoya University Living Guide explains how your spouse and children can join you, how to register them at the ward office, and where to find day care and schools. See the [Family page](https://life.gmc.nagoya-u.ac.jp/en/life-in-japan/family/) and the [family section of Living in Nagoya](living-in-nagoya).
