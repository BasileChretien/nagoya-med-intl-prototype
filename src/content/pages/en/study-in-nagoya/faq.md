## Can I apply if my school is not a partner?

For clinical clerkships, not at present. The office accepts applications from students of partner medical schools only and will update the website if this changes. Write to the Office of International Affairs if you have a question about your school.

## Who can do a clinical clerkship?

Enrolled senior medical students of a fully accredited medical school, in good standing, with enough clinical clerkship experience and a letter of approval from the Dean. You need to be fluent in Japanese or English.

## How long can I stay?

Students of partner schools who apply directly can stay from one week to 90 days, with at least two weeks in one department. Through NUPACE you can stay from 91 days to one year, with at most three months of clinical clerkship. Research training usually lasts one to four weeks.

## How much does it cost?

Students of partner schools with a tuition waiver agreement pay no tuition, registration or admission fees. The number of waivers is limited. Otherwise tuition is 29,600 yen for each four weeks. You also pay for your insurance and your living costs.

## Is there a scholarship?

NUPACE students who are full-time students at their home school can apply for a JASSO scholarship of 80,000 yen a month, plus a one-time allowance of 80,000 yen. Very few places are available.

## When should I apply?

Contact the office first to confirm availability. Complete documents are due six months before the start of your clerkship. NUPACE applicants follow the date on the NUPACE website.

## Can I choose my department or contact it myself?

Please do not contact departments or faculty members directly. All enquiries go through the Office of International Affairs, which coordinates your rotation.

## Do I receive credits?

No. You receive a certificate of attendance, and your home school decides how to process it.

## Can I work part time?

No. You must not take side jobs during the clerkship or the research program.

## Where can I stay?

A dormitory is available when there are vacancies. Ask the office when you apply.
