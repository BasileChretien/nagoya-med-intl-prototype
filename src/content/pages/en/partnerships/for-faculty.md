This page is for faculty members and clinicians of Nagoya University Graduate School of Medicine and School of Medicine, and of the University Hospital. The Department of International Medical Education, whose professor also directs the Office of International Affairs, gives as its first mission the promotion of international joint research by the Graduate School of Medicine.

## Starting an international collaboration

The Office of International Affairs coordinates the school's agreements with institutions abroad. It also runs the school's part in the [GAME](game) and [MIRAI](mirai) networks, organises joint symposia and receives delegations.

If you want to work with a university, hospital or institute abroad, or turn an existing contact into an agreement, write to the office. Say who the partner is, what you plan (research, student exchange, joint education) and which department is involved. The [agreements page](agreements) shows whether the institution is already a partner. [Becoming a partner](become-a-partner) shows what a partner institution is asked to send.

GAME projects are led by member schools, and Nagoya leads the international cohort of people at risk of Lewy body disease. To join a GAME project or reach the people in charge of one, ask the office.

## What other offices handle

- **Contracts with companies, intellectual property and clinical trials.** See [For companies and research institutes](companies) and the faculty's [corporate page](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_J/company/) (in Japanese).
- **Security export control.** Before you invite someone from abroad, check the university's rules on [receiving people from abroad](https://www.aip.nagoya-u.ac.jp/securityexport/reception) (in Japanese).

## Hosting a researcher or student from abroad

- **Research students and visiting researchers.** See [Visiting researchers, postdocs and PhD applicants](../study-in-nagoya/visiting-researchers) and [Research training for international students](../study-in-nagoya/research-training).
- **Visa.** For a visitor who comes as a student, the university applies in Japan for the Certificate of Eligibility. It takes about one month, so start early.
- **UNC scholars.** The scholar exchange with the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill works in both directions. See [Scholar exchange with UNC Chapel Hill](../degrees/unc-scholar-exchange).
- **Life in Nagoya.** For housing, insurance and daily life, see [Living in Nagoya](../study-in-nagoya/living-in-nagoya).

## Funding

- The Japan Society for the Promotion of Science runs [Invitational Fellowships](https://www.jsps.go.jp/english/e-inv/), for overseas researchers with strong records who come for joint research, and [Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://www.jsps.go.jp/english/e-fellow/), for pre- and post-doctoral researchers who work under a host in Japan.
- The university's research office helps with [applications for external funding](https://www.aip.nagoya-u.ac.jp/academic-research/gaibu-support) and supports [international conferences](https://www.aip.nagoya-u.ac.jp/academic-research/convention) (both in Japanese).
- MIRAI 2.0 offered seed funding for joint projects between its member universities. See [MIRAI](mirai) for its current status.

## Working with the School of Health Sciences

The departments of medicine and of health sciences hold joint lectures in English, and students of both take part together in the medical English programme. See [Medical English classes and training](../study-abroad/medical-english) and the [School of Health Sciences](https://www.met.nagoya-u.ac.jp/ENGLISH/index.html).
