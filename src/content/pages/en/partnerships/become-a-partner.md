This page is for universities, medical schools, research institutions and hospitals abroad that would like a formal partnership with Nagoya University Graduate School of Medicine and School of Medicine. The Office of International Affairs coordinates the school's agreements, so it is the place to start.

## Kinds of partnership

The school's partners are linked to it in one or more of these ways. The [agreements page](agreements) shows which applies to each partner.

- **Academic exchange agreement.** An agreement between the two institutions on academic exchange. It is the basis for exchange of students and for contacts between researchers. The faculty's table lists such agreements from 1989 to 2019.
- **Student exchange.** Partner medical schools send senior students to Nagoya for clinical clerkships, and Nagoya students go to partner schools for clinical electives.
- **Tuition fee waiver agreement.** Students of a partner school that has concluded this agreement with Nagoya University pay no tuition, registration or admission fees for a clerkship.
- **Joint and double degrees.** Doctoral programmes with Adelaide, Lund and Freiburg (joint degrees) and with The Chinese University of Hong Kong (double degree). See the [Degrees section](../degrees/index).
- **Networks.** A medical school can take part in the [GAME](game) alliance or the [MIRAI](mirai) consortium without a bilateral agreement.

## What an agreement changes for your students

At present the clinical clerkship at Nagoya accepts only students of partner schools. Partner students can also stay longer, and may come through NUPACE, the university-wide exchange programme. The [Clinical clerkship](../study-in-nagoya/clinical-clerkship) page gives the current rules, periods and costs.

If you are a student at a school that is not a partner, ask your school's international office or dean's office to contact the Office of International Affairs.

## How to start

1. Write to the Office of International Affairs. The address is on the [Contact](../about/contact) page.
2. In your first message, say which institution and faculty you represent, who will be responsible for the partnership, and what kind of cooperation you propose (student exchange, research, joint education). Mention any Nagoya faculty members you already work with.
3. Ask the office about the next steps, the documents it needs and how long approval usually takes.

As an example of a signing, the dean of Nagoya University School of Medicine and the dean of National Taiwan University College of Medicine signed their exchange agreement in Taiwan on 17 June 2011.

## Visiting before you sign

You can also visit the school before you propose an agreement. See [Delegation visits](visits) to arrange a visit.
