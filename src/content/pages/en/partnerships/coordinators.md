This page is for the international office or coordinator of a partner school. It explains how your students come to Nagoya and what to settle with the Office of International Affairs. Students themselves will find the full procedure on the [Clinical clerkship](../study-in-nagoya/clinical-clerkship) page.

## Sending students to Nagoya

- **Ask first.** Places are limited. The student, or you on their behalf, writes to the office to confirm that a place is available before sending any documents. The message gives the student's name, the school and its country, and the intended period.
- **The Dean's letters.** The student needs a written letter of approval from the Dean of your school or the Dean's authorized representative. The application also includes a letter of recommendation from the Dean or the representative. It states that the student is a senior student in good standing with at least half a year of clinical clerkship at your school.
- **Two routes.** For a shorter stay, the student applies to the office. For a longer stay, the student applies through NUPACE, the university-wide exchange programme, with the clerkship documents attached. The clerkship page gives the periods of each route. See also the [NUPACE website](https://nupace.iee.nagoya-u.ac.jp/en/index.shtml).
- **Deadlines and documents.** The clerkship page lists the documents and the deadline.
- **Tuition.** Students of a partner school with a tuition fee waiver agreement pay no tuition, registration or admission fees. Ask the office whether your school has this agreement and how many waivers it covers.

## What to ask the office

The office can tell you the number of places for your school, the periods when students can come, and the departments that accept visiting students. Write to it through the [Contact](../about/contact) page. Include the name of your school and the agreement concerned.

For a new agreement, or to change or renew an existing one, see [Becoming a partner](become-a-partner).

## Nagoya students coming to you

Nagoya students go to partner schools for clinical electives and research. See [Overseas clinical clerkship dispatch program](../study-abroad/clinical-electives).
