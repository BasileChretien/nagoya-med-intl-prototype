This page is for companies, research institutes and other organisations that want to work with Nagoya University Graduate School of Medicine. Several offices share this work. The list below says which one to contact.

## Who handles what

- **Joint research, contracted research, academic consulting, donations and endowed chairs.** The faculty's [corporate page](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_J/company/) (in Japanese) links to a page for each of them. The forms and model contracts for joint research are on the [joint research page](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_J/company/iagc/collabo/).
- **International collaboration with industry.** The university's Academic Research and Industry-Academia-Government Collaboration office has a [page on international industry-academia collaboration](https://www.aip.nagoya-u.ac.jp/sanren/industry-kokusai), in English and Japanese, with its contact for joint research.
- **Clinical trials.** The Department of Advanced Medicine of Nagoya University Hospital has a [page for trial sponsors](https://www2.nu-camcr.org/client/) (in Japanese).
- **International education and exchange.** The Office of International Affairs handles the school's partnerships with universities, hospitals and research institutions abroad, delegation visits and international education programmes. Research contracts go through the faculty's corporate page and the contract office described below. Write to it through the [Contact](../about/contact) page if your question concerns these programmes.

## How joint research starts

The faculty's [joint research page](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_J/company/iagc/collabo/) (in Japanese) describes these steps.

1. You discuss the project with the Nagoya faculty member who will lead it on the university side.
2. You and that faculty member prepare an application and a draft contract and send them to the contract office.
3. If the research is clinical research that needs ethics review, the university's ethics committee must approve it before the application.
4. The contract office checks the documents with the intellectual property and legal offices. An internal meeting, such as the faculty council, reviews the application, and the head of the faculty or unit decides whether to accept it.
5. Both sides sign the contract, and the company pays the research costs on the university's invoice.

## Finding a research partner

The [laboratory directory](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_E/laboratory/) lists the departments and laboratories of the Graduate School of Medicine. The [Industry-Academia Collaborative Chairs page](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_E/laboratory/industrial-collabo/) lists the chairs run with companies, in fields such as skin science and stem cells, brain imaging and AI, cell therapy for solid cancer, and MRI diagnosis.

Two areas linked to the Office of International Affairs are:

- the international cohort of people at risk of Lewy body disease, a [GAME](game) project led by Nagoya University;
- the [Cancer Immune Therapy Research Center](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/cancerimmuno/www/en/index.html), which works on oncolytic viruses and on cell therapy with genetically modified T cells against advanced cancer.
