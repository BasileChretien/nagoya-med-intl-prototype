Nagoya University holds joint symposia with partner universities to present research and to prepare joint education. They are hosted in turn, and each is usually paired with a meeting on cooperation. The meetings are listed from the oldest to the newest.

## Symposia with Adelaide and Freiburg (FAN series)

The University of Adelaide, the University of Freiburg and Nagoya University meet in turn at one of the three. The numbering starts with a symposium between Nagoya and Adelaide in 2013. These meetings prepared the joint degree programmes with Adelaide and Freiburg.

| Date | Number and place | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 27 May 2013 | 1st, Adelaide | Nagoya and Adelaide only. Ten researchers from Nagoya, led by the dean, presented work on neurological diseases and cancer. |
| 18 March 2014 | 2nd, Nagoya (Tsurumai campus) | Cooperation meetings on 19 and 20 March. The three universities signed a memorandum that includes a joint doctoral degree programme. |
| 22 September to 3 October 2014 | 3rd, Adelaide | Five Nagoya doctoral students presented at the Florey International Student Research Conference. A meeting on the joint degree programme was held. |
| 15 to 17 October 2015 | 4th, Nagoya (Kakuyu-kaikan, Tsurumai campus) | The second symposium hosted by Nagoya. Thirteen researchers presented, and students presented for the first time. |
| 17 and 18 October 2016 | 5th, Adelaide | Thirteen researchers presented. Two Nagoya students spent two weeks in a laboratory. |
| 6 October 2017 | 6th, Freiburg | Held with a meeting on how to run the joint degree programmes. |
| 22 and 23 November 2018 | 7th, Nagoya (Kakuyu-kaikan, Tsurumai campus) | The first symposium of the four-university alliance with Lund University, called FLAN. |

## A related visit

From 7 to 14 March 2015, a Nagoya delegation took part in the second International Conference on Cell Motility in Freiburg. It discussed joint research with Freiburg and prepared the October 2015 symposium in Nagoya.

## Numbering

The October 2015 symposium was the fourth of the series and the second that Nagoya hosted, after the one in March 2014. The numbers in the table follow this count.
