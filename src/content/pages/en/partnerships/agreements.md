The School of Medicine has agreements with universities, research institutions and hospitals abroad. The Office of International Affairs coordinates them. They allow students to exchange, researchers to work together, and the school to join networks and joint degree programmes.

As of March 2025, the school lists 29 medical schools with an inter-school academic exchange agreement. Their students can apply for a clinical clerkship at Nagoya. The list below is wider: it also includes hospitals, nursing schools, research institutes, a ministry, and universities linked to Nagoya only through a network.

## How to read the list

- **Year.** The year the first agreement with the partner was signed, as given in the faculty's agreements table. The oldest is the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill (1989) and the newest the University of Leicester (2019). Partners that the table does not list have no year.
- **Region and country.** Partners are grouped by region, then listed by name.
- **Tags.** What links the partner to Nagoya, explained below.

## What the tags mean

Each partner in the list carries one or more tags.

- **Agreement.** A bilateral agreement is listed on the faculty site's agreements table or on the exchange site.
- **Clerkship.** The partner is on the March 2025 list of schools whose students can apply for a clinical clerkship at Nagoya University Hospital. See [Clinical clerkship](../study-in-nagoya/clinical-clerkship).
- **GAME** and **MIRAI.** The partner belongs to one of the two networks. See [GAME](game) and [MIRAI](mirai).
- **JDP.** The partner runs a joint degree programme with Nagoya.
- **DD.** The partner offers a double degree with Nagoya.
- **SEP.** The partner takes part in the scholar exchange program.

A partner can belong to a network without a bilateral agreement, so some entries carry no Agreement tag.

## Joint programmes

Joint degree programmes are held with Adelaide University, Lund University and the University of Freiburg. The double degree is with The Chinese University of Hong Kong and the scholar exchange with the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. The [Degrees section](../degrees/index) describes them.

## Hospital partnership

Nagoya University and its hospital also have a partnership with Bangkok Hospital in Thailand. The exchange site describes exchanges of staff and the referral and transfer of patients. The partnership also helps students and staff of the university if something unexpected happens to them in Asia. Bangkok Hospital appears in the list without a tag for this reason.
