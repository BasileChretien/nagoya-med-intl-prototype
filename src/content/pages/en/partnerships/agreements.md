The School of Medicine has agreements with universities, research institutions and hospitals abroad. The Office of International Affairs coordinates them. They allow students to exchange, researchers to work together, and the school to join networks and joint degree programmes. The oldest agreement in the faculty's list is with the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill (1989) and the newest with the University of Leicester (2019).

## What the tags mean

Each partner in the list carries one or more tags.

- **Agreement.** A bilateral agreement is listed on the faculty site's agreements table or on the exchange site.
- **Clerkship.** The partner is on the list of schools whose students can apply for a clinical clerkship at Nagoya University Hospital.
- **GAME** and **MIRAI.** The partner belongs to one of the two networks. See [GAME](game) and [MIRAI](mirai).
- **JDP.** The partner runs a joint degree programme with Nagoya.
- **DD.** The partner offers a double degree with Nagoya.
- **SEP.** The partner takes part in the scholar exchange program.

A partner can belong to a network without a bilateral agreement, so some entries carry no Agreement tag.

## Joint programmes

Joint degree programmes are held with Adelaide University, Lund University and the University of Freiburg. The double degree is with The Chinese University of Hong Kong and the scholar exchange with the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. The Degrees section of this site describes them.
