MIRAI is an academic alliance between Swedish and Japanese universities. Its aim is to tackle global issues together, to promote research, education and innovation between the two countries, and to train the next generation of academics. It grew out of the Japan-Sweden University Presidents' Summit of October 2015. In 2017, seven Swedish and eight Japanese universities founded it, with support from the Swedish Foundation for International Cooperation in Research and Higher Education (STINT).

## Nagoya's role

Nagoya University has been one of the main members since the founding and is the coordinating university on the Japanese side. The University of Gothenburg coordinates on the Swedish side. In the School of Medicine, Branko Aleksic of the Office of International Affairs takes a central role and works across disciplines.

## MIRAI 2.0, 2020 to 2022

After the first phase, the universities met in Tokyo in 2018, agreed to continue and issued a joint statement on the value of Sweden-Japan collaboration. The next phase, MIRAI 2.0, started in 2020 with more member universities and four themes: aging, material sciences, sustainability and AI. The old pages give its period as 2020 to 2022. They list these activities:

- a Research and Innovation Week for young researchers;
- seed funding for joint projects between member universities, for researchers who found a partner at the Research and Innovation Week;
- short courses for PhD students and postdoctoral researchers;
- mobility of faculty and administrative staff, with financial support;
- student exchange between member universities.

The old pages give MIRAI 2.0 the period 2020 to 2022. The latest event they list is the Digital Research and Innovation Week of June 2021. They show no event, call or seed funding after 2022, and announce no next event. Ask the office whether MIRAI continues and how to take part.

## Members in MIRAI 2.0

Sweden: University of Gothenburg (coordinating), Linköping University, Lund University, Uppsala University, Umeå University, Linnaeus University, Karlstad University, Örebro University, Jönköping University, Luleå University of Technology, and the Stockholm School of Economics as an associate member.

Japan: Nagoya University (coordinating), Hiroshima University, Hokkaido University, Sophia University, Kyushu University, The University of Tokyo, Tohoku University and Waseda University.

## Past events

- **7 and 8 October 2020.** A digital kick-off for MIRAI 2.0, organized by Nagoya University and the University of Gothenburg.
- **7 to 11 June 2021.** The Digital Research and Innovation Week, hosted by the University of Gothenburg.
- **13 and 14 November 2019.** The MIRAI Seminar, with parallel sessions on aging, sustainability, materials science and innovation.
- **March to June 2019.** Three workshops, on the Sustainable Development Goals (Tokyo), materials science (Linköping) and innovation and entrepreneurship (Tokyo).
- **11 and 12 October 2018.** The MIRAI Seminar in Tokyo on a sustainable social system and technology for an aging society.
- **May to June 2018.** Workshops on aging (Chalmers University of Technology), materials science (Kyushu University) and sustainability (University of Gothenburg).
- **16 to 19 May 2017.** The MIRAI Seminar, with parallel sessions on innovation, aging, materials science and sustainability.

Short courses for PhD students were also held in 2018 and 2019 in Sweden and Japan, on subjects such as sustainable mobility, membranes for water and magnetic properties of materials.
