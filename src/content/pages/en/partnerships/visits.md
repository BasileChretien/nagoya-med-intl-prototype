Nagoya University welcomes delegations from universities, hospitals and research institutions abroad. The Office of International Affairs helps visitors who want to meet representatives of the School of Medicine or see its facilities.

## How to request a visit

Send your request to the Office of International Affairs. The contact details are on the contact page of the About section. The office can do the following.

- Exchange messages with your coordinator and prepare the documents needed to receive you.
- Issue official documents from the university when you need them.
- Arrange a courtesy meeting with school representatives, and visits to facilities.

A meeting with a representative may not always be possible because of schedules. Send your enquiry as early as you can, with details about your group and what you wish to do. The office has experience with delegations from many countries, and its staff work with visitors in English.

## Recent visits

The school's reports describe these visits.

**2026**

- 5 February. A professor from the University of Western Australia gave a hybrid special lecture on engineering approaches to diseases that medicine cannot cure.

**2025**

- 9 January. The director of the bioinformatics research center at North Carolina State University met faculty to discuss data-driven life science.
- 3 April. Two staff of the international office of the Medical University of Vienna visited. They reviewed the exchange since the 2005 agreement, in which 28 medical students came to Nagoya and 25 went to Vienna for clerkships.
- 19 May. The Associate Provost for Global Affairs of the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill discussed the summer program and researcher exchange.
- 26 June. A delegation from the Medical University of Gdansk, a partner since 1995, met the university and school leaders.
- 27 November. Two faculty members of Mayo Clinic Florida discussed research exchange and a possible internship for Nagoya students.

**2024**

- 27 February to 1 March. A geriatrics researcher from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill spent four days meeting researchers in geriatrics and public health.
- 7 March. A professor from Monash University took part in a mini-symposium at Nagoya University Hospital.
- 15 March. A delegation of seven from the University of Nottingham, led by its president, met university and school leaders.
- 28 March. A professor from the University of Freiburg visited and spoke. The joint degree agreement was under renewal talks.
- 12 and 13 September. Visitors from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and the University of Freiburg toured the hospital and met faculty.
- 10 December. A delegation from the University of Reims Champagne-Ardenne, led by its vice president for medical AI, discussed cooperation in medical AI.

**2023**

- 12 December. A delegation from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, led by its vice provost for global affairs, met university and school leaders.

Earlier reports on the exchange site cover visits in 2018 and 2019 by delegations from Mongolia, Korea, Hong Kong, Germany, Thailand, China, Taiwan, Uzbekistan and the United Kingdom.
