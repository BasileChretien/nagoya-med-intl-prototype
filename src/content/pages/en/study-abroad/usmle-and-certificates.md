This page is for Nagoya University medical students and graduates who take the USMLE or apply for residency in the United States. The graduate school student affairs section (大学院係) of the Graduate School of Medicine handles two procedures: confirming your student status for ECFMG, and issuing the MSPE.

The official instructions, with the section's email address, are on the faculty page [procedures for a US medical license](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_J/international/application/license/) (in Japanese).

## Applying for the USMLE

Nagoya University is registered on EMSWP, ECFMG's web registration site for medical schools. You can therefore apply to ECFMG for the USMLE through OASIS. Read ECFMG's instructions carefully. Information on the exams is on the [USMLE website](https://www.usmle.org/).

## Status and credentials verification

While you apply to ECFMG for the USMLE, Nagoya must confirm your student status on the ECFMG site.

Write to the graduate school student affairs section from your university email address. Use the subject 「USMLE受験申請について: 氏名（所属）」, with your name and affiliation. Give your year and name, state that you are applying to ECFMG for the USMLE, and ask for verification of your student status. The section checks the ECFMG site and confirms your status as soon as ECFMG sends its request.

Approval takes time, so ask early.

## MSPE for a US residency application

The MSPE (Medical Student Performance Evaluation) is the AAMC document that residency programs use to assess your clinical training and academic record. Nagoya issues it, in the name of the Dean, to graduates who need it for a US residency application.

**Deadline.** Send your draft by the end of May of the year of your match. For the 2025 match, for example, the deadline was the end of May 2025. Writing the MSPE takes at least one month after your request is accepted.

1. Request and pay online. Use the university's online certificate service (see [certificates for graduates](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_J/alumni/issue/), in Japanese). Graduates register the first time they use it; students log in directly. Choose "other certificates" (その他の証明書) for the MSPE. If ECFMG also needs a new Medical School Transcript, select it at the same time. Fees are not refunded.
2. Write a draft in English. Follow the faculty's [MSPE format guide](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_J/international/pdf/d032ef90b26c98f53f28064d6cc5a41f4fd67f6b.pdf) (PDF). You write section 2 (Noteworthy Characteristics, up to three items of up to two sentences each), section 4 (Academic Progress, only the clinical course and elective rotations) and section 5 (Summary). The university writes sections 1, 3 and 6. Write only facts the Dean can verify.
3. Collect evidence for sections 2 and 4. For example, records of exchange programs, awards, qualifications, presentations and papers, and English test scores. Add an English caption to documents in Japanese. If you joined an exchange program and have no document for it, say so in your email, and the section will check with the Office of International Affairs.
4. Email the section once the fee is paid. Use the subject 「MSPE 発行依頼: 氏名（所属）」. Give your name, your student number if you know it, a telephone number and an email address, and state that you have applied for the MSPE. Attach the draft as a Word file and the evidence as PDF or similar files.
5. Stay reachable. The section checks your draft against university records and then writes the MSPE. It may contact you to confirm facts.
6. The section uploads the finished MSPE, the evidence and the Medical School Transcript to ERAS.
