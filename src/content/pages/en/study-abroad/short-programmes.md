These programs fit into summer or spring breaks. Many do not run on a fixed yearly schedule, so check the latest information with the Office of International Affairs. Before you leave, read [safety, insurance and emergencies](safety).

## GAME TEI Summer Camp

The Transnational Educational Initiative (TEI), led by Korea University, brings medical students from around the world to a summer camp. Nagoya University belongs to GAME (Global Alliance of Medical Excellence) and takes part.

The first camp took place at Nagoya in 2019. It lasted five days, and its themes were disaster medicine and professionalism. Eighteen students from seven universities took part. They visited Fukushima to see hospitals and the reconstruction work, and they toured a disaster prevention center in Nagoya and the university's Mitigation Center. The second camp is listed for 2020 at Bologna and the third for 2021 at Monash. The third camp was led by Monash University and held online in the evenings of 26 to 29 July 2021.

No later camp has been announced.

## Entrepreneurship programs

### International entrepreneurship program with the University of Colorado Boulder

Nagoya undergraduate and graduate students of any faculty can join. The program has an online course in March and an in-person course in May at Nagoya University. You form a team with US students, use design thinking to build a business idea in health care or health tech, and give a final presentation.

Twenty students are selected. The program is free, with support for travel and stay, and carries no credit, but you receive a certificate of completion. It is taught in English, so you need sufficient English.

In 2025, applications closed on 31 January. The online course ran from 5 to 20 March and the in-person course from 17 to 24 May. The next dates are not set.

### Overseas entrepreneurship program (Shibahara Fund)

This program is supported by the Keiichi Shibahara Fund. A short stay is about one month in a vacation, and a long stay is up to one year on leave of absence. Four students are supported a year, three short and one long. Support is up to JPY 3 million for a short stay and up to JPY 8 million for a long one. You can join an entrepreneurship program at a university or institution abroad, or an internship at a company abroad. See [scholarships](scholarships).

## Short clerkship at UK medical schools

The Medical Education Promotion Foundation runs a four-week clinical clerkship at six UK universities. It is open to fifth-year students who have taken the IELTS Academic module and who have finished about a year of clinical clerkship. Nagoya plans to recommend two students.

The foundation handles the call and selection, so check its website. Nagoya had one recipient each in 2019, 2020, 2023 and 2024.

## Exchange with St John's College, Cambridge

This is a two-week program of lectures in your field and cultural exchange, without credit. Up to six students are chosen by internal selection.

You must be enrolled in a regular course, able to follow lectures and join discussions in English, and hold a cumulative GPA of 3.7 or higher. You also help organize cultural events when Cambridge students visit Nagoya, at your own expense. The travel period is not set.
