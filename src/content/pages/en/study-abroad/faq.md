## How do I start?

Contact the study-abroad adviser at the Office of International Affairs. The periods when you can go and the programs open to you are very limited, and an application takes effort, so please ask early. Advice is available at any time.

## How much money do I need?

It depends a lot on the program and the country. For clinical clerkships abroad, tuition is normally waived under the agreements. Once you know which program you want, ask the office for an estimate.

## What programs are there?

They differ by English level and learning goal. The main ones are listed on the [study abroad overview](index). Tell the office what you want and they will help you find a fit.

## I am not confident in English. Can I still go abroad?

Most students who enter already have a solid grounding in basic English. They have few chances to use it, though, and many lose confidence. Some programs accept students who are not confident in English, and they can also be a good chance to study it. Please take the first step.

## Which English test do you recommend?

TOEFL and IELTS are widely accepted by universities around the world. If you plan to go to the UK, IELTS is especially recommended.

## Can I stay abroad for a long time?

A long stay is very hard to fit into the medical curriculum. Credits cannot be transferred, so you would need to take a leave of absence.

## Do I get credit for a clinical clerkship abroad?

Yes. Up to three months of clerkship abroad is recognized as credit at Nagoya University, and you do not need to make it up. See [clinical electives](clinical-electives).

## Can anyone receive a scholarship?

Scholarships have financial and academic criteria, and only those who meet them can receive one. Check the criteria of the scholarship you apply for. Which programs are covered also differs by scholarship, so ask the office. See [scholarships](scholarships).

## Can I receive more than one scholarship?

Often you cannot, and it differs by scholarship. Your financial situation can also reduce the amount or make you ineligible. Ask the office for details.

## Whom should I ask?

The office advises undergraduates, graduate students and graduates. The contact box on this page shows how to reach them.
