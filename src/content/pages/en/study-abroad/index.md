Medical students of Nagoya University can study abroad in three ways: through a university program, through certified study abroad (認定留学), or as a private student (私費留学). The Office of International Affairs gives advice on all three.

## Main routes

University programs send you to partner universities and similar institutions. The main ones are:

- [Clinical electives abroad](clinical-electives). You train abroad for up to three months, between January of the fifth year and April of the sixth year.
- [Research abroad](research-abroad). You take part in basic-science research at a university or research institute abroad.
- [Short programs](short-programmes). These include summer camps, entrepreneurship programs, a short clerkship in the UK and an exchange with Cambridge.
- [Medical English classes and training](medical-english). These are classes at Nagoya and English courses abroad.

You can also go abroad outside the university programs, as a certified or private student. The office gives advice and information in this case too.

## In years 1 to 4

You do not have to wait for the clerkship of the fifth year: some options are open earlier. The basic medical seminar abroad, for third-year students, is described on [research abroad](research-abroad). Medical English at the University of Leicester is open to all years. At Nagoya, you can take elective medical English from the first year, and the special elective on developing an international outlook in the fourth year. These three are described on [medical English classes and training](medical-english).

There are also summer and entrepreneurship programs. For example, undergraduates of any faculty can join the program with the University of Colorado Boulder. See [short programs](short-programmes).

Talk to the office early. It helps you choose what fits your year and your English.

## For doctoral students

In the [scholar exchange with UNC](../degrees/unc-scholar-exchange), you do research at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill for up to one year. With [joint and double degrees](../degrees), you take a doctoral degree together with a partner university abroad.

The two weeks of lectures and exchange with St John's College, Cambridge, are open to graduate students in a regular course. The entrepreneurship program with Colorado is open to graduate students of any faculty. Both are described on [short programs](short-programmes).

Whether you need a leave of absence, and whether a Nagoya scholarship continues during your stay, depends on your situation. Ask the office before you apply.

## How the office helps

You can ask for advice at any time. The study periods and programs open to you are limited, and an application takes effort, so please come early.

The office helps you find a program that fits your goals and English level. Scholarships inside and outside the university, both grants and loans, are described on [scholarships](scholarships). To support language study, the office runs preparation courses for language tests and lends reference books. Its study-abroad seminars help you think about life abroad and afterwards and settle your worries.

For students going on clinical clerkships, the office runs pre-departure training in English on Saturdays. A crisis-management seminar is held before departure. See [safety, insurance and emergencies](safety), which also has a summary for parents. At the report meeting each August, returned students present by university.

A long stay abroad is hard to fit into the medical curriculum. Credits cannot be transferred, so you would need to take a leave of absence.
