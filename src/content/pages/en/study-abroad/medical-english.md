You can learn medicine in English before you go abroad, both at Nagoya and in joint online classes. This page covers classes at Nagoya, joint online classes and courses abroad.

## Classes and training at Nagoya

- Developing an international outlook. A special elective for fourth-year students. Faculty from Japan and abroad help you form an international outlook and set goals, and you discuss in English with international students.
- Basic medical English. A lecture for the whole year group. If you have not studied medical English before, it starts with the basics, such as how to memorize medical vocabulary. International students introduce health care in their countries, and you ask questions and discuss.
- Elective medical English. Open to first- to fifth-year students. A foreign physician teaches medicine in English, from basic to clinical topics, following your year's curriculum.
- Clinical simulation (PALS session). Simulation training in pediatric emergencies, held in English. A Nagoya graduate at Children's Hospital of Philadelphia runs the simulator remotely.
- Pre-departure training. Students going on clinical clerkships abroad take it in English on Saturdays. Physicians with training experience abroad teach it, and recently returned students join to support juniors.

## Joint online classes

### LMU-NU USMLE review classes

These classes use questions from the US medical licensing exam (USMLE) and are for upper-year students. A foreign faculty member teaches them every other Friday at 3 pm. USMLE Step 2 CK classes began in July 2016 for fourth- and fifth-year students, and since November 2020 students of the University of Munich and Gifu University have joined.

Nagoya students are informed of the schedule through NUCT. If you are an undergraduate who wants to join, contact the office. For status verification with ECFMG and the MSPE, see [USMLE and certificates](usmle-and-certificates).

### GAME online clinical case discussion

Students of GAME universities discuss clinical cases in English. Each time, faculty of a participating university set a clinical topic and the students discuss the case and treatment strategy. It is for motivated upper-year students, free of charge, and you receive a certificate of participation.

Three editions have been held. The first was in November and December 2020, with 16 students from four universities. The second ran from 25 May to 29 June 2021 with 23 students from six universities. The third ran from 24 May to 5 July 2022 as a series of seven sessions. No next edition has been announced.

### Online medical English with the University of Leicester

This online course focuses on medical interviews. Leicester's English teaching unit runs it, and in the last session you interview a simulated patient in English. Seven students took part in November 2020 and 14 in March 2021. Future editions are not set.

## English courses abroad

Before you leave for a course abroad, read [safety, insurance and emergencies](safety).

### Medical English at the University of Leicester

Leicester holds a three-week intensive medical English course for medical students. It is supervised by the British Council, includes visits to local hospitals and lectures by health professionals, and uses small classes grouped by level.

All years can join, and Nagoya students need no English score. The fee is GBP 1,800 for tuition and the dormitory, and travel is extra. Dates and the internal deadline are not set, so ask the office.

### Intensive medical English in Hawaii

This course at Hawaii Tokai International College trains medical English and case presentation. It is for fifth- and sixth-year students with English around TOEFL iBT 80 (TOEIC 730).

The cost is about USD 1,350, covering lodging, tuition, local transport and weekday breakfast and lunch. Flights are not included. The period and deadline are not set. Submit your application documents and CV to the office.
