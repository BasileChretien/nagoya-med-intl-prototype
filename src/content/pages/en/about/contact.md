The contact boxes at the end of this page give the email address, telephone number and postal address of the two offices. You can write in English or Japanese. To come in person, see [How to reach the office](access), and write first so that someone is there to meet you.

## Who we are

The Office of International Affairs handles the international work of the Graduate School of Medicine and School of Medicine. Its head is Professor Hideki Kasuya, Vice Dean for International Affairs. The team of faculty members and coordinators is listed in the faculty's [laboratory directory](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_E/laboratory/clinical-med/comprehensive-ambulatory/international-medical-education/). Write to the office address rather than to a staff member.

## Find your topic

### Coming to Nagoya

- **Clinical clerkship.** Read [Clinical clerkship in Nagoya](../study-in-nagoya/clinical-clerkship), then write to the Office of International Affairs. Do not write to departments or faculty members to apply. All enquiries go through the Office of International Affairs.
- **Research training for students.** Read [Research training](../study-in-nagoya/research-training), then write to the office.
- **Researchers and doctors.** Read [Visiting researchers](../study-in-nagoya/visiting-researchers). If a Nagoya faculty member has already agreed to host you, write to the office and copy your host.
- **Joint degree, double degree or scholar exchange.** Write to the joint degree address. See [Degrees](../degrees).

### Nagoya students and graduates

- **Study abroad.** Read [Study abroad](../study-abroad), then write to the Office of International Affairs.
- **USMLE and certificates for residency abroad.** See [USMLE and certificates](../study-abroad/usmle-and-certificates).
- **Certificates for graduates.** The faculty's [alumni page](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_E/alumni/) explains how to request them.
- **Former incoming students.** If you need a certificate or letter about a clerkship or research stay you did in Nagoya, write to the office.

### Partners, companies and donors

- **Partner universities.** For a new agreement or a renewal, read [Become a partner](../partnerships/become-a-partner). Exchange coordinators can see [For coordinators](../partnerships/coordinators). Then write to the office.
- **Delegation visits.** Read [Delegation visits](../partnerships/visits). Write early, because meetings with school leaders depend on their schedules.
- **Companies.** Read [Companies](../partnerships/companies), then write to the office.
- **Donations.** Read [Support our programmes](support). It explains how to give and which faculty office handles gifts.

### Media and official enquiries

Write to the Office of International Affairs for questions about the school's international programmes and partnerships. To interview or film faculty members, the faculty asks you to apply with its press and filming request form. The form is on the faculty's [media page](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_J/others/media/), in Japanese. The General Affairs Section handles it.

### Patients

For appointments and treatment, go to the [Nagoya University Hospital website](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/hospital_en/). The hospital has a [guide for foreign patients without Japanese public health insurance](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/hospital_en/guide/outpatient/overseas/).
