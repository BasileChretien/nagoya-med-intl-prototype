You can reach the office by email, by telephone or in person. The contact boxes on this page give the current email address, telephone number and postal address.

## Which contact to use

- **Office of International Affairs.** Use it for clinical clerkships and research stays at Nagoya, study abroad for Nagoya students, partner universities, delegation visits and general questions.
- **Joint degree programmes.** Use the joint degree address for the joint degree programmes, the double degree with CUHK and the scholar exchange with UNC.

If you want to apply for a clinical clerkship, write to the office. Do not contact individual faculty members or clinical departments directly. All enquiries go through the office.

## Where the office is

The office is on the Tsurumai campus of Nagoya University Graduate School of Medicine and School of Medicine, in Showa Ward, Nagoya. The old pages offer a map with directions from Tsurumai Station to the office.

## Delegations and partner schools

To arrange a visit, see the Delegation visits page in the Partnerships section. Write early, because meetings with school leaders depend on their schedules.
