## Donations

Nagoya University accepts donations of money or securities from companies, organizations and individuals, to improve its research and education. A gift can support any of the purposes on the faculty's [donation page](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_J/company/iagc/donation/), which is in Japanese. One of them is exchange in education and research with universities abroad, and support for international students. The others include scholarships for students and the research and teaching environment.

The Keiichi Shibahara Fund, set up by a graduate of the School of Medicine, already pays for students' study abroad. See [Scholarships](../study-abroad/scholarships). If you would like to support the international programmes, write to the Office of International Affairs first, through the [contact page](contact). Ask the office which programmes a gift could support.

### How to give

1. Send the donation application form (Form 1) to the Research Support Section of the Management Planning Division, in the administrative office of the School of Medicine and Graduate School of Medicine. The form is on the donation page.
2. The faculty council or another review body examines the gift and decides whether to accept it.
3. The faculty sends you a letter of thanks and a payment request. Pay with that request.
4. The faculty sends your receipt in the month after your payment, about one month later.

A fixed share of each gift pays for the running costs of education and research.

### Conditions you can set

You can name the group of students a gift supports, or the research it pays for. You can also ask for a short report on the results, a summary of income and spending, and the return of any money left once the purpose is complete.

The university does not accept a gift that gives the donor property bought with it or rights to the results of the research. It also refuses a gift that lets the donor audit its use, lets the donor cancel it after applying, or creates a financial burden for the university.

### Tax

Under the Corporation Tax Act of Japan, a company can deduct the whole gift as an expense. An individual can receive a tax benefit under the Income Tax Act, and local inhabitant tax may also be reduced.

### Whom to ask

- **Procedure, forms and receipts.** The Research Support Section: its email and telephone are in the box below, and its fax number is on the donation page.
- **Supporting an international programme.** The Office of International Affairs. Its address is on the [contact page](contact).

## Alumni of study abroad

Students who had studied abroad founded the Frontier-kai in 1998, when they were students. It is a student-run alumni association. Its aims are to share information and to help the students who come after them. For more than 20 years it has brought together doctors who did a clinical elective abroad as Nagoya medical students.

The association's representative also teaches students who are preparing to go abroad, for example how to examine patients and write medical records in English. The association's page is on the office's [exchange site](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/japanese/support/exchange/association.html), in Japanese. If you are a graduate who studied abroad and want to help, write to the Office of International Affairs through the [contact page](contact).

For other services for graduates, such as certificates, see the faculty's [alumni page](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_E/alumni/).
