## What the site does

- Every page exists in English and Japanese, and the language switch opens the same page in the other language.
- Every page can be used with a keyboard. The first link on each page skips straight to the content.
- Every photo has a description in both languages for screen readers.
- Acronyms are spelled out where they first appear and listed at the end of each page.
- Links to a page in the other language, and links to PDF files, say so.
- The route to the office is described in words on the [access page](access), as well as on a map.

## What has been tested

On 25 November 2026, the automated accessibility audits of Lighthouse 12 scored 100 out of 100 on all ten pages tested, on a phone-sized screen. Automated tests find only part of the problems a person can meet. The site has not yet been tested by people who use screen readers or other assistive technology.

## Known limits

Many links lead to the faculty's pages and to other sites, which this site does not control. Some of those pages exist only in Japanese. Some documents there are PDF files that can be hard to read with a screen reader.

## Report a problem

If something on this site is hard to use, write to the office through the [contact page](contact). Say which page it was and what happened.
