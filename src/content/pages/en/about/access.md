The office is on the Tsurumai Campus of the School of Medicine, next to Nagoya University Hospital. The building, floor and room are in the box below.

## From the station

- **JR Chuo Line, Tsurumai Station:** leave by the Meidaibyoin Exit (on the hospital side). About 3 minutes on foot. Start at step C below.
- **Subway Tsurumai Line, Tsurumai Station:** leave by Exit 4. About 8 minutes on foot. Start at step A below.

The Meidaibyoin bus stop is also marked on the route map.

## Step by step

The letters match the route map and the photos in the printable route guide.

1. A: Come up to street level from subway Exit 4. The JR line is on your left.
2. B: Walk along the JR line, keeping it on your left, with Tsurumai Park on your right.
3. C: Pass the Meidaibyoin Exit of JR Tsurumai Station on your left.
4. D: You reach the Tsurumai Campus of the School of Medicine, with the multi-storey car park on your right.
5. E: Pass the front gate of the hospital on your right.
6. F: When you see the car shop on your left, turn right.
7. G: Enter the campus by the West Gate and go straight.
8. H: At the Basic Medical Research Building, turn left.
9. I: Enter the building by the automatic door on the left and go up to the 2nd floor. The office is directly above the university co-op shop.

For the whole campus, see the faculty's [access and campus map](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_E/access/) page.
