The International Collaborative Program in Comprehensive Medical Science between Nagoya University and the University of Freiburg (Nagoya = Freiburg JDP) was established in June 2018. It aims to train research leaders who promote international collaboration and act as a bridge between the EU and Japan.

## Capacity

With Nagoya University as the principal university, 1 student is admitted each year. One more student a year enrols with the University of Freiburg as their principal university, which makes 2 in total.

## Who can apply

Graduates of the medical schools of Nagoya University or the University of Freiburg are the main target. Graduates with a bachelor's degree in dentistry, pharmacy or veterinary medicine, and holders of a master's degree in engineering, agriculture or medicine, can also apply. If your basic medical knowledge is judged insufficient, you may be asked to take foundation courses that do not count toward the credits.

English, preferably shown at application and judged at the interview: TOEFL iBT 100 or higher with at least 25 in each section, or IELTS Academic with Overall 7.0 or higher and at least 7.0 in each section.

## Structure

You need 30 credits.

- Group A, Nagoya: International Collaborative Advanced Medicine (2), seminar (10) and experimental research (6).
- Group B, Freiburg: seminar (10) and experimental research (2).

Because the degree is a joint one, you can also join the activities of Freiburg doctoral students, as long as they do not take time from your own experiments.

- Monthly reunions, 10 to 12 times a year. Doctoral students present results in 15 minutes and discuss them for 5.
- Annual retreats, a residential training event each September with guest lectures and poster sessions.
- Oberseminars, interdisciplinary literature seminars.

## Time in Freiburg

You spend 12 months at the University of Freiburg, in principle in the second year. The stay can be extended to a maximum of 30 months if the experiments need special techniques or the research plan changes. You agree the period with your supervisors.

## Completing the degree

- Stay enrolled for four years and spend 12 months in Freiburg.
- Earn 30 credits.
- Write a dissertation and pass the examination. At least one article must be accepted in a peer-reviewed international journal.

The examination is held once at Nagoya University. The dean of the partner faculty and your partner supervisor join by video conference. You receive a Doctor of Philosophy in Medical Science as a single joint degree.
