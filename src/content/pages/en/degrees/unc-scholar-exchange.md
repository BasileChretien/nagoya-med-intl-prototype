The Scholar Exchange Program (SEP) between Nagoya University Graduate School of Medicine and the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill (UNC) School of Medicine exchanges graduate students and young researchers for a fixed period. UNC is the oldest public university in the United States. The two universities have talked about collaborative research and a possible joint degree programme in medicine in the future.

The key facts above give the duration, places, support and deadline. To compare this programme with the joint degrees and the double degree, see the table on [Degree programmes](index).

## What you do

You are accepted by the partner university and carry out research in one of its laboratories, in the field you choose, with the support of a UNC faculty member. At the end you receive an evaluation and a certificate of completion from the partner university. You do not receive a UNC degree. Research that looks toward future joint work is encouraged, and the results can be used as part of your doctoral dissertation at your own university.

## How long you stay

You can stay up to one year. The programme funds stays of at least 3 months, and a stay of 6 months or more is preferred.

## Who can apply from Nagoya

You need to intend to keep doing research after you return, and to be one of the following:

- A graduate student in the medical doctoral programme.
- A lecturer or assistant professor of the Graduate School of Medicine or the University Hospital, aged 39 or younger, except hospital faculty. Faculty members can only be sent to promote joint research between their laboratory and the overseas institution, not for self-improvement.
- A paid researcher of the Graduate School of Medicine or the University Hospital, aged 39 or younger, who has completed a doctoral course at the Graduate School of Medicine.

The age is counted on 1 April of the fiscal year in which your stay starts.

## Language

At application you must meet one of the following UNC requirements, with the test taken in the last two years: TOEFL iBT 65, IELTS 6, CEFR B2, OPI Intermediate Mid or TOEIC 650.

## Support

Doctoral students receive a grant for travel and return and a monthly allowance for the stay. Faculty members and researchers can keep their Nagoya salary during the stay. No tuition is payable at UNC.

## How to apply

Selection is ongoing through the year.

1. Ask the joint degree programmes office through the [contact page](../about/contact) for the two UNC forms, the visiting scholar application form and the recommendation form.
2. Prepare a CV in English and a motivation letter, in any format. These two documents are forwarded to UNC.
3. Send all four documents to the joint degree programmes office before the deadline in the key facts above.
