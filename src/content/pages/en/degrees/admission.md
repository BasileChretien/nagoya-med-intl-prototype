This page is for applicants who enrol with Nagoya University as their principal university. The double degree with CUHK and the scholar exchange with UNC have their own pages.

## Places and intakes

Each year 2 students are admitted to the Adelaide programme, 2 to the Lund programme and 1 to the Freiburg programme. Applications for April enrolment are invited every year. October enrolment is opened only if places remain.

You can apply to the joint programme and to the general doctoral course (Program in Integrated Medicine) at the same time. The examination fee is JPY 30,000 whether you apply to one or both, and each course judges you separately.

## Timeline

For April enrolment, the usual pattern is as follows.

1. Before applying, consult the Office of International Affairs, your department and the graduate school office, and take an English test such as TOEFL or IELTS.
2. Apply in early June of the year before enrolment. You may apply to the general doctoral course at the same time.
3. Interviews, with Nagoya University and with the partner university, from July to September.
4. The result from Nagoya is announced from September to November, and the final result from both universities after that.
5. Enrol in April and start your studies at Nagoya.
6. Study abroad for more than 12 months, from April of the following year or later.
7. Return to Nagoya and continue your research.

For October enrolment, applications are in early April, interviews from May to July and results from July to September.

The exact dates for the next intake have not been published yet. They are announced in the call for applications on the graduate school website. The last dated cycle on the old site was applications in June 2017 for April 2018 enrolment, which no longer applies.

## Documents

The call for applications on the graduate school website lists the documents to submit to Nagoya University. After the interview you also submit documents to the partner university.

- Adelaide University: graduation certificate and academic transcript in English, a CV with research history, papers and presentations, a research proposal, and the agreement of your proposed supervisor.
- Lund University: Lund's application form, graduation certificate in English, a CV, and a study plan made with your proposed supervisor.

## Transfer

Students of the general doctoral course can apply to transfer to the joint programme in their first year. You must pass the joint programme interview and finish the procedure within that year. Transfer in the third year is not possible. Students of the joint programme can move to the general course if they pass its examination, with no penalty.

## Fees

You pay fees to Nagoya University only. Tuition at the partner university is waived.

- Entrance fee: JPY 282,000.
- Tuition: JPY 267,900 per half year, JPY 535,800 a year.

These are the same as for the general doctoral course. If fees are revised while you are enrolled, the new amounts apply from the revision.

## Support for the stay abroad

The JDP incentive, revised in April 2025, has two parts.

- Travel: up to JPY 300,000, paid once. It is not paid if another scholarship covers your travel.
- Research activities abroad: JPY 250,000 a month for 12 months. If your stay is longer than 12 months, the period can be extended to a maximum of 24 months after review.

You may need to apply for designated scholarships. The Tokai National Higher Education and Research System also offers a programme for doctoral students, the THERS Make New Standards Program. If you receive a Japanese government scholarship, ask the graduate school office first.

## Housing

Adelaide University publishes student accommodation information on its website. Lund University arranges housing for your first year there. Housing for Freiburg is not described on the old site.
