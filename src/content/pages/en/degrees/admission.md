This page is for applicants who enrol with Nagoya University as their principal university. The double degree with CUHK and the scholar exchange with UNC have their own pages. Places, costs and the status of the next call for every programme are in the table on [Degree programmes](index).

## Intakes

Applications for April enrolment are invited every year. If the April places are not filled, a call for October enrolment follows.

You can apply to the joint programme and to the general doctoral course (Program in Integrated Medicine) at the same time. The examination fee is JPY 30,000 whether you apply to one or both, and each course judges you separately.

## Where the call is published

Each call for applications is published on the graduate school's [call page for the joint degree programmes](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_J/admission/doctral/admissions/international/) (in Japanese). The page has the guidelines, the application forms and the form for the eligibility pre-screening.

In 2024 to 2026 the calls for April enrolment were posted in April and July, and the calls for October enrolment in February to April. The status of the next call for each programme is in the table on [Degree programmes](index). Ask the joint degree programmes office through the [contact page](../about/contact) for the dates of the next round.

## Timeline

For April enrolment, the JDP admission page gives this pattern.

1. Before applying, consult the joint degree programmes office, your department and the graduate school office, and take an English test such as TOEFL or IELTS.
2. Apply in the year before enrolment. You may apply to the general doctoral course at the same time.
3. Interviews, with Nagoya University and with the partner university.
4. The result from Nagoya is announced first, and the final result from both universities after that.
5. Enrol in April and start your studies at Nagoya.
6. Study abroad for more than 12 months, from April of the following year or later.
7. Return to Nagoya and continue your research.

## Documents

The call for applications lists the documents to submit to Nagoya University. After the interview you also submit documents to the partner university.

- Adelaide University: graduation certificate and academic transcript in English, a CV with research history, papers and presentations, a research proposal, and the agreement of your proposed supervisor.
- Lund University: Lund's application form, graduation certificate in English, a CV, and a study plan made with your proposed supervisor.

## Transfer

Students of the general doctoral course can apply to transfer to the joint programme in their first year. You must pass the joint programme interview and finish the procedure within that year. Transfer in the third year is not possible. Students of the joint programme can move to the general course if they pass its examination, with no penalty.

## Fees

You pay fees to Nagoya University only. Tuition at the partner university is waived. The amounts below are those published in 2025.

- Entrance fee: JPY 282,000.
- Tuition: JPY 267,900 per half year, JPY 535,800 a year.

These are the same as for the general doctoral course. If fees are revised while you are enrolled, the new amounts apply from the revision.

## Support for the stay abroad

The JDP incentive, as revised in April 2025, has two parts.

- Travel: up to JPY 300,000, paid once. It is not paid if another scholarship covers your travel.
- Research activities abroad: JPY 250,000 a month for 12 months. If your stay is longer than 12 months, the period can be extended to a maximum of 24 months after review.

You may need to apply for designated scholarships. If you receive a Japanese government scholarship, ask the graduate school office first.

The Tokai National Higher Education and Research System (THERS) also offers the [THERS Make New Standards Program for the Next Generation Researchers](https://dec.nagoya-u.ac.jp/phd-mns/en/). It gives selected doctoral students financial support and career support, so that they can focus on their research. It is run with the Japan Science and Technology Agency (JST). Calls for applications are announced on its website.

## Housing

Adelaide University publishes [student accommodation information](https://adelaide.edu.au/life-at-adelaide/accommodation/) on its website. Lund University arranges housing for your first year there. For housing in Freiburg, ask the joint degree programmes office.
