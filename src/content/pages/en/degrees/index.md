The Graduate School of Medicine offers three kinds of programme for doctoral students and young researchers who want to spend a long period of research at a university abroad. They differ in the degree you receive and in how long you stay. The table at the bottom of this page compares all five programmes side by side: who can apply, duration, places, degree, cost, support and the next call.

## The three types

**Joint degree.** You follow one jointly designed doctoral programme with one research project and two supervisors, one at each university. At the end you receive a single degree issued in the names of both universities. You stay at the partner university for at least 12 months within the usual four years. Nagoya University offers joint degrees with Adelaide University, Lund University and the University of Freiburg. Their official names follow the pattern "Nagoya = Adelaide JDP", where JDP stands for joint degree programme. See [Joint degree programmes](joint-degree).

**Double degree.** You enrol in the doctoral programmes of both universities and receive a separate degree from each. You write two dissertations with different content. Nagoya University offers this with The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK). See [Double degree with CUHK](cuhk-double-degree).

**Scholar exchange.** You carry out research in a laboratory of the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill (UNC) for three months to one year, and receive a certificate of completion. You do not receive a UNC degree, and the results can become part of your own doctoral dissertation. See [Scholar exchange with UNC](unc-scholar-exchange).

## Which one suits you

Choose a joint degree if you want an internationally recognised single degree and a stay of a year or more with a partner supervisor. Choose the double degree if you want a doctorate from each of two universities and are ready to write two dissertations. Choose the scholar exchange if you want a shorter stay, or if you are a young faculty member or researcher rather than a degree candidate.

For how to apply, see [Admission, fees and support](admission) and the [questions and answers](faq). For any of these programmes, write to the joint degree programmes office through the [contact page](../about/contact).

## If you are at a foreign university

### You study at a partner university

The joint degrees, the double degree and the scholar exchange each take students from both sides. If you want Adelaide University or Lund University as your principal university, apply through the postgraduate studies office of your own university. If you study at Freiburg, CUHK or UNC, ask the joint degree programmes office how to apply.

### You want Nagoya University as your principal university

Graduates of other universities can apply to the joint degrees if they hold a qualifying degree (see the page for each programme). You apply to Nagoya University through the graduate school's [call for applications](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_J/admission/doctral/admissions/international/) (in Japanese). The call page also has the form for an eligibility pre-screening.

### You want a regular PhD in Nagoya

The doctoral course of the Graduate School of Medicine admits international students. The application guidelines are on the faculty's [Doctoral Admission](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_E/admission/doctral/) page. Two routes give a Japanese government (MEXT) scholarship.

- Embassy recommendation: you apply from your own country through the Japanese embassy or consulate there, and arrive in Japan in April or October.
- Medical Science Research Leader programme: a special programme that runs for three years from 2025, through which the university recommends outstanding students who already live in Japan for a MEXT scholarship. For October 2025 entry it had 5 places and required a GPA of 2.30 or higher. Ask the Office of International Affairs through the [contact page](../about/contact) whether a new call is open.

### You want to do research without a degree

You can come as a research student. See the faculty's [Research Student Admission](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_E/admission/research/) page and [Visiting researchers](../study-in-nagoya/visiting-researchers).
