The International Collaborative Program in Comprehensive Medical Science between Nagoya University and Lund University (Nagoya = Lund JDP) was established in November 2016. Lund University in Sweden is among the top 100 universities worldwide, and its medical research is strong in neuroscience, especially neurodegenerative disease.

## Who can apply

Graduates of the medical schools of Nagoya University or Lund University are the main target. Graduates with a bachelor's degree in dentistry, pharmacy or veterinary medicine, and holders of a master's degree in engineering, agriculture or medicine, can also apply. If your basic medical knowledge is judged insufficient, you may be asked to take foundation courses that do not count toward the credits.

English, shown at the time of application: TOEFL iBT 79 or higher, or IELTS Academic with Overall 6.5 or higher and at least 5.0 in each section.

## Structure

You need 48 credits if you were admitted from 1 January 2024. One Nagoya credit equals 45 hours of study and corresponds to 1.5 ECTS at Lund.

- Group A, Nagoya: International Collaborative Advanced Medicine (2), Applied Statistics I and II (3), Portfolio A (4), seminar (10), experimental research (6).
- Group B, Lund: Introduction to Research Methodology (2), Research Ethics (2), Oral Communication (1), Theory and practice of scientific communication (1), one elective (1), Portfolio B (4), seminar (10), experimental research (2). If you work with laboratory animals, the elective must be Laboratory animal science for researchers.

The portfolio records your progress each year and is discussed with your supervisor. Nagoya supervisors assess Portfolio A and Lund supervisors assess Portfolio B.

## Time in Lund

You spend at least 12 months at Lund University, from the second year of the doctoral course. A longer stay, up to 30 months, can be approved by the joint committee when the experiments need special techniques or the host laboratory is highly specialised.

Past example study plans include neuroscience in Lund's Multipark research environment, working on Parkinson's disease, and health and welfare research for engineering graduates.

## Completing the degree

- Stay enrolled for four years and spend at least 12 months at Lund.
- Earn 48 credits and complete the half-time review at Lund, carried out by supervisors and an external committee.
- Write a dissertation and pass the examination. You normally need at least three related papers. One must be published or accepted and you must be its sole first author. On the others you must be first or second author.

The examination is held once at Nagoya University, with Lund's examiners and your partner supervisor joining online. You receive a Doctor of Philosophy in Medical Science as a single joint degree. Lund University arranges housing for your first year there.
