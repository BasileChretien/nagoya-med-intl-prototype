## Recruitment

**Is there a recruitment every year?**
April enrolment is offered every year. October enrolment is offered only if places were not filled in April, so applying for April is strongly encouraged.

**When can I apply?**
For April enrolment, applications are usually in early June of the previous year. For October enrolment, they are in early April. Dates can change, so check the call for applications or ask the office. See [Admission, fees and support](admission).

## Eligibility

**I have no English test score. Do I need one when I apply?**
In principle the score must be attached to your application. If a test date falls soon after the deadline, consult the office first. Good English is essential for doing research at the partner university, so keep improving it.

**My score is too low or has expired.**
Consult the office first. After the first screening, the partner university may ask for an online meeting to judge your English.

**I am in the third year of my doctorate. Can I move to the joint programme?**
No. Transfer in the third year is not possible. Students in the first year of the general doctoral course can apply to transfer, if they pass the joint programme interview.

## Fees

**Do I pay fees to both universities?**
No. You pay admission and tuition fees to Nagoya University only. The partner university's tuition is waived.

## Enrolment and supervisors

**If my laboratory sends a student, do we have to accept a partner student in return?**
No. Accepting a student is not a condition. If the partner university makes a request in future, please consider it as far as you can.

**Can I do any specialty?**
Both partner universities have many departments across a wide range of research. The office tries to find a host laboratory that matches your theme.

**I work in a clinical department. Can I research in the same clinical field abroad?**
Most laboratories at the partner universities can take part. If your first choice is not possible, a basic medicine or radiology department may be an option.

**I have no supervisor at the partner university. Can I still apply?**
Yes. First search the partner university's website and staff lists, and tell the office if you find someone. The Office of International Affairs also helps you find a host laboratory. Before enrolment there are online meetings to plan the joint research project.

## During the programme

**I am worried about my progress, because I cannot do research in Nagoya while I am abroad.**
The research is carried out jointly by both universities, and you continue your own project while at the partner university. The goal is to bring the work together.

For other questions, contact the Office of International Affairs.
