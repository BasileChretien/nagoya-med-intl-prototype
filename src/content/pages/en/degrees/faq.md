## Recruitment

**Is there a recruitment every year?**
April enrolment is offered every year. October enrolment is offered only if places were not filled in April, so applying for April is strongly encouraged.

**When can I apply?**
Each call is published on the graduate school's [call page](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medical_J/admission/doctral/admissions/international/) (in Japanese). The status of the next call for each programme is in the table on [Degree programmes](index). See also [Admission, fees and support](admission).

## Eligibility

**I have no English test score. Do I need one when I apply?**
In principle the score must be attached to your application. If a test date falls soon after the deadline, consult the joint degree programmes office first. Good English is essential for doing research at the partner university, so keep improving it.

**My score is too low or has expired.**
Consult the joint degree programmes office first. After the first screening, the partner university may ask for an online meeting to judge your English.

**I am in the third year of my doctorate. Can I move to the joint programme?**
No. Transfer in the third year is not possible. Students in the first year of the general doctoral course can apply to transfer, if they pass the joint programme interview.

## Fees

**Do I pay fees to both universities?**
No. You pay admission and tuition fees to Nagoya University only. The partner university's tuition is waived.

## Enrolment and supervisors

**If my laboratory sends a student, do we have to accept a partner student in return?**
No. Accepting a student is not a condition. If the partner university makes a request in future, please consider it as far as you can.

**Can I do any specialty?**
Both partner universities have many departments across a wide range of research. The joint degree programmes office tries to find a host laboratory that matches your theme.

**I work in a clinical department. Can I research in the same clinical field abroad?**
Most laboratories at the partner universities can take part. If your first choice is not possible, a basic medicine or radiology department may be an option.

**I have no supervisor at the partner university. Can I still apply?**
Yes. First search the partner university's website and staff lists, and tell the joint degree programmes office if you find someone. The office also helps you find a host laboratory. Before enrolment there are online meetings to plan the joint research project.

## During the programme

**I am worried about my progress, because I cannot do research in Nagoya while I am abroad.**
The research is carried out jointly by both universities, and you continue your own project while at the partner university. The goal is to bring the work together.

## Living in Nagoya and abroad

**Do I need a visa?**
If you live abroad and come to study in Nagoya, you need a student visa. Nagoya University applies for your Certificate of Eligibility, and you then apply for the visa at the Japanese embassy or consulate near you. The university's [visa guide](https://life.gmc.nagoya-u.ac.jp/en/arrival/visa/) explains each step. For your stay at the partner university, ask the joint degree programmes office which visa you need.

**Where will I live?**
In Nagoya, international students who are new to Japan can live in a university dormitory if they meet certain conditions. You apply in January for April and in July for October. Most students may stay 6 months to 1 year, then move to a private apartment; see the university's [housing guide](https://life.gmc.nagoya-u.ac.jp/en/life-in-japan/housing/). Abroad, Adelaide University publishes [accommodation information](https://adelaide.edu.au/life-at-adelaide/accommodation/), and Lund University arranges housing for your first year. For Freiburg, ask the joint degree programmes office.

**How much does it cost to live in Nagoya?**
The university's [pre-arrival guide](https://life.gmc.nagoya-u.ac.jp/en/arrival/preparation/) estimates the monthly costs of a single student. Rent for a private apartment is about JPY 35,000 to 60,000, food about JPY 45,000 and utilities about JPY 10,000. It advises you to bring enough money for at least two months. While you are abroad, the JDP incentive pays a monthly allowance for your research; see [Admission, fees and support](admission).

**Can my family come with me?**
Your spouse and the children you support can join you in Japan with the "Dependent" status of residence. You need a stable income to support them, and you apply for their Certificate of Eligibility. The university's [family guide](https://life.gmc.nagoya-u.ac.jp/en/life-in-japan/family/) also covers registration, day care and schools. If your family will go with you to the partner university, ask the joint degree programmes office early.

For other questions, write to the joint degree programmes office through the [contact page](../about/contact).
