Nagoya University Graduate School of Medicine and the School of Medicine of The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) run a double (dual) degree programme for doctoral students. You study at both universities and receive a doctoral degree from each when you meet each university's requirements.

## The partner

CUHK is a public university founded in 1963. Exchanges with Nagoya University began in 2010, and the two medical schools signed an agreement in 2016 for an exchange of clinical training for medical students. CUHK is also the organising school of the Global Alliance of Medical Excellence (GAME), of which Nagoya University is a member.

## Requirements for Nagoya students

- You receive a doctoral degree from each university when you complete the programme.
- The term of study at Nagoya is four years. At CUHK it is three years if you hold a master's degree and four years if you do not.
- You must do research at Nagoya for at least the first year after enrolling.
- You spend half of CUHK's term of study doing research at CUHK.
- Up to 10 credits can be transferred. The compulsory subjects at CUHK total 50 credits.
- Start and end dates do not have to be the same at both universities.
- You write two dissertations with different content. CUHK's thesis does not have to be published.
- You make a course plan when you enrol, and submit an annual progress report to each university.
- If you withdraw from Nagoya, you are also withdrawn from CUHK.

## Eligibility

You must be enrolled, or about to enrol, in the doctoral programme of the Graduate School of Medicine at Nagoya University (general medicine course). You must be academically excellent and of good conduct. You must also meet one of these language requirements at the time of application, with the test taken in the last two years: IELTS 6.5, TOEFL 79 or DMAT 21.

## Costs and support

You pay the entrance examination fee, admission fee and tuition to Nagoya University, in the same amounts as the general medicine course. You also pay CUHK an entrance examination fee of HK$300 (about JPY 5,700).

While you are at CUHK you pay tuition of HK$42,100 a year (about JPY 800,000) and can receive a scholarship of HK$224,700 a year (about JPY 4.26 million). These are the amounts for 2024-2025 and they can change from year to year.
