# International

The Office of International Affairs runs the international programmes of the School of Medicine. Full information, deadlines and how to apply are on the international website.

[Open the international website](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/)

## [Study in Nagoya](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/study-in-nagoya/)

| Programme | Who it is for |
| --- | --- |
| [Clinical clerkship for overseas students](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/study-in-nagoya/clinical-clerkship/) | Senior medical students of partner schools |
| [Program for International Research Students](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/study-in-nagoya/research-training/) | Enrolled undergraduate and graduate students from abroad |

## [Study abroad](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/study-abroad/)

| Programme | Who it is for |
| --- | --- |
| [Basic medical seminar overseas dispatch program](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/study-abroad/research-abroad/) | Third-year medical students |
| [Short student exchange with St John's College, University of Cambridge](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/study-abroad/short-programmes/) | Undergraduate or graduate students enrolled in a regular course |
| [Overseas clinical clerkship dispatch program](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/study-abroad/clinical-electives/) | Nagoya University medical students who apply in the fifth year and train abroad during Clinical Clerkship II |
| [International entrepreneurship education program with the University of Colorado Boulder](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/study-abroad/short-programmes/) | Nagoya University undergraduate and graduate students of any faculty (the 2025 call also asked for sufficient English) |
| [GAME online joint clinical case discussion](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/study-abroad/medical-english/) | Senior medical students who are motivated |
| [Intensive summer medical English course in Hawaii (HTIC)](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/study-abroad/medical-english/) | Fifth- and sixth-year medical students with about TOEFL iBT 80 (TOEIC 730) |
| [Medical English summer school at the University of Leicester](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/study-abroad/medical-english/) | Medical students of all years; no English score required for Nagoya students |
| [LMU-NU collaborative online USMLE review classes](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/study-abroad/medical-english/) | Upper-year medical students (the classes began in 2016 for fourth- and fifth-year students) |
| [Elective clinical clerkship in basic-science departments abroad](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/study-abroad/research-abroad/) | Fifth- and sixth-year medical students with strong research motivation |
| [Overseas entrepreneurship dispatch program (Shibahara Fund)](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/study-abroad/short-programmes/) | Nagoya University medical students |
| [Keiichi Shibahara Fund overseas study support program for medical students](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/study-abroad/scholarships/) | Nagoya University medical students in four overseas programs |
| [GAME TEI Summer Camp](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/study-abroad/short-programmes/) | Medical students of GAME partner universities |
| [Short clinical clerkship at UK medical schools (Medical Education Promotion Foundation)](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/study-abroad/short-programmes/) | Students entering the final year (currently fifth-year) who have taken the IELTS Academic module and have completed about one year of clinical clerkship |

## [Joint & double degrees](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/degrees/)

| Programme | Who it is for |
| --- | --- |
| [Double (Dual) Degree Program between Nagoya University Graduate School of Medicine and The Chinese University of Hong Kong, School of Medicine](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/degrees/cuhk-double-degree/) | Doctoral students of the Graduate School of Medicine, Nagoya University, who are enrolled or about to enrol |
| [International Collaborative Program in Comprehensive Medical Science between Nagoya University and Adelaide University (Nagoya = Adelaide JDP)](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/degrees/adelaide/) | Doctoral applicants with a medical degree, or a dentistry, pharmacy or veterinary bachelor's degree, or a master's degree in engineering, agriculture or medicine |
| [International Collaborative Program in Comprehensive Medical Science between Nagoya University and the University of Freiburg (Nagoya = Freiburg JDP)](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/degrees/freiburg/) | Doctoral applicants with a medical degree, or a dentistry, pharmacy or veterinary bachelor's degree, or a master's degree in engineering, agriculture or medicine |
| [International Collaborative Program in Comprehensive Medical Science between Nagoya University and Lund University (Nagoya = Lund JDP)](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/degrees/lund/) | Doctoral applicants with a medical degree, or a dentistry, pharmacy or veterinary bachelor's degree, or a master's degree in engineering, agriculture or medicine |
| [Scholar Exchange Program (SEP) between Nagoya University Graduate School of Medicine and the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, School of Medicine](https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/intlexch/en/degrees/unc-scholar-exchange/) | Medical doctoral students, and lecturers, assistant professors and paid researchers aged 39 or younger, who intend to continue research after returning |
